# BaseRoll User Guide

## For Companies

### 1. Register Your Company
- Connect wallet (MetaMask)
- Click "Register Company"
- Fill in company details
- Confirm transaction

### 2. Add Employees
- Navigate to "Employees"
- Click "Add Employee"
- Enter employee details and wallet address
- Set salary and payment token
- Confirm on-chain transaction

### 3. Process Payments
- Go to "Payments"
- Select employees
- Click "Process Payment"
- Confirm transaction

## For Employees

### View Payment History
- Connect your wallet
- View payment history in dashboard
- Export to CSV if needed

## Troubleshooting

- Ensure you're on Base network
- Check wallet has sufficient funds for gas
- Contact support if issues persist
